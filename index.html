🌈 Kids English Poems

Learn English with Fun, Poems and Pictures!

☀️ Good Morning

Good morning, sun so bright, Fill the world with golden light. Birds are singing in the tree, Good morning to you and me!

🌳 Nature

Trees are green and skies are blue, Flowers bloom for me and you. Rivers sing and breezes play, Nature makes a lovely day!

🐦 Little Bird

Little bird up in the sky, Spread your wings and fly so high. Sing a song for everyone, Play beneath the golden sun!

🌈 Rainbow

Rainbow shining in the sky, Seven colours way up high. After rain, you come to play, Making smiles throughout the day!

🌸 My Garden

Flowers dancing in the breeze, Butterflies among the trees. My little garden shines so bright, A lovely and colourful sight!

❤️ My Family

My family is kind and dear, They always make me smile and cheer. Together we laugh and play, I love my family every day!
